Go-to-market plan for the promoter

The opportunity

Until 2030, Advait is the only licensed senior home in Gurugram a family can move a parent into.

The OC is due in December and keys in March 2027. The next licensed homes, JMS Silver Living and Antara at Estate 360, are due around 2030; Antara at Estate 361 and DLF's The Aureva around 2033. About 50 homes are left. This plan sells them while that window is open.

The risk

Almost nobody is hearing about it. Four of Advait's five live Meta ads show under 100 impressions. Google shows 4 Advait ads to Antara's 22. Searches for "Advait senior living" are too low to register, and a Hyderabad project called Advait Living runs senior-living ads under the same name.

The window narrows. The only ready senior homes in NCR are Antara Noida, across the city, and older stock in Sohna. Haryana raised the floor-area ratio for retirement housing to 3.0 in April 2026, and Colliers expects 75,000 new senior units in 3–4 years. More licensed supply is coming to Gurugram. None of it can deliver before Advait.

How families actually search.

Monthly Google searches

India · seodata.dev estimates

Families search for care, not for "senior living". "Old age home gurgaon" is the phrase with volume. The industry's own words barely register.
Antara is searched by name about 5,000 times a month. Advait isn't searched by name at all yet. Brand search has to be built, and defended.
Care-intent search is cheap. Cost per click on the "old age home" cluster runs under a dollar. It's the most direct route to a family that is deciding now.

What it means

Local seniors buying for themselves are the core. Gurugram and Delhi children are the second engine. NRI children are the growth engine nobody in NCR is working, and the one most tied to the December–January visit window.

What the price says.

Priced for a home, not a trade

₹18,500 BSP is 7% above Nirvana Country's own flats. The premium is the licence and the care. Resale is 55+ only, so there's no investor case.

Far below the brands

Antara at Estate 361 is ~₹22,500 and The Aureva ₹28,000–32,000 per sq ft. Never name them in ads. Lead with care and timing.

The care-home check

Type B's all-in earns ~₹2.46 L a month in a 7% deposit, above Gurugram's dearest care home. Ownership wins on home, title and family, not on cost.

Split · two quarters

Why the money goes where it goes.

Local first

About 65% of buyers so far live in Gurugram. Meta in Gurugram and South/West Delhi, plus print and WhatsApp for seniors who trust the newspaper, take ~42% of the budget.

NRI is a test that can scale

NRI cost per lead ranges ₹3,300–13,200 between vendors. We start at ₹12 L, read cost per site visit after four weeks, then scale or cut.

Referrals cost least

Events, the CK Birla MoU and an owner-referral programme take ~14%. They should deliver a quarter or more of bookings.

Budget excludes Fixit's fee and channel-partner brokerage. Reallocated weekly inside the approved total on cost per site visit. Brokerage benchmark 2–3% for luxury (vendor sources); marketing spend rule of thumb 1–3% of sales value (unaudited). Remaining stock value at the weighted price list.
